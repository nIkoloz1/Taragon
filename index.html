How it works

From a messy POS file to insight you can act on.

You hand us the export your accounting system already produces. We do the rest — detect the format, parse it, recognise the brands, and match each line to a verified product record.

1

Ingest & understand

Drop any POS export — legacy .xls, .xlsx or CSV. We fingerprint the layout, handle grouped categories, and structure every row automatically.

2

Match to the world

Each product is matched against a master database built from open, legally-licensed data — by brand, pack size and name similarity — even across languages and scripts.

Every match gets a confidence grade so you know what to trust at a glance, then causal analysis shows what's actually driving your sales and margins.
